Application that utilizes **Open Trivia DB API**, **node js**, and **express**.  Weighted scores based on how fast you answer questions, enter your name after the quiz to be added to the (**Pantry**) database.  If you place in the top 10, your score will be highlighted to represent your position among the leaders.
